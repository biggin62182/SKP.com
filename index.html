What we do

Getting people where they need to be — safely, on time, every day.

Safe Keepers Place provides professional passenger transportation for school districts, specialized education programs, and private clients. Whatever the route, the standard is the same.

School district routes

Daily home-to-school routes, mid-day runs, and activity trips under contract with districts across the metro. Consistent drivers, consistent times.

Specialized & private programs

Transportation for students in therapeutic, autism, and alternative education settings — where patience, routine, and a familiar face matter as much as the schedule.

Senior transportation

Dependable rides for older adults to appointments, programs, and standing weekly commitments — with drivers who take the time to help at both ends of the trip.

Private & charter trips

Groups, events, and one-off trips for private clients. As the family has always put it: safe transportation of people, anytime, anywhere.
